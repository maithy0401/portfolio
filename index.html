FLEX-DATAProduct OwnerLOTTE C&F Vietnam · 2025–26

Making a multi-step BNPL onboarding measurable

LOTTE FLEX is LOTTE C&F Vietnam's Buy Now Pay Later app. To get a credit limit, a new user verifies their phone number, passes identity verification (eKYC), adds more information and signs a contract. I owned the tracking plan and the reporting that shows where people drop out.

Role
Product Owner responsibilities, UI/UX
Worked with
Mobile developers, BA, Business teams
Scope
Onboarding, from splash screen to credit limit
Tools
GA4, Google Sheets, Jira, Confluence

The question

Onboarding is where a credit app wins or loses a customer, and every step has a regulatory reason to exist. The team needed a reliable answer to one question: at which step do applicants drop out, and why?

What I did

  1. Wrote the tracking plan: main events, popup and warning events, funnel milestones and a parameter dictionary, under one naming convention.
  2. Worked with mobile developers on the implementation, then checked every event against live GA4 data.
  3. Built four funnels and eighteen supporting reports on top of the right custom definitions.
  4. Wrote setup guides so Business and BA can rebuild and read the reports without me.

Decisions I made along the way

D-01

The SDK always said yes

Found

The eKYC SDK returns no pass or fail status, so the app logged every attempt as a success.

Decided

Stop measuring pass rate from the SDK callback. Use the result event, which reports both true and false.

Trade-off

One fewer client-side signal, in exchange for a pass rate that counts failures.

D-02

A ten-step ceiling

Found

GA4 funnels stop at ten steps, and the full onboarding has more milestones than that.

Decided

Start the master funnel at the splash screen to catch early exits, and move reference-contact detail into its own funnel.

Trade-off

The master view is coarser; the detail is one funnel away.

D-03

A segment that would hide the drop

Found

A "new registrants only" segment relies on the phone check event, which people who leave at splash never fire.

Decided

No segment on the master funnel. Keep it on the three stage funnels.

Trade-off

The master includes some returning users, but no longer hides the earliest exits.

D-04

Data the app could not collect

Found

The SDK version could not be collected, so the planned breakdown of eKYC errors was impossible.

Decided

Drop the parameter. Break errors down by app version, with a version map from the mobile team.

Trade-off

One extra lookup step, and the report still answers the question.

From the work

Outcome

The team has one view of onboarding from the first screen to the credit limit, a dictionary that developers, BA and business read the same way, and an eKYC pass rate that counts failures.

How I work

From goal to release, and back again

The loop I run for each feature at LOTTE FLEX. Design is one step of six.

  1. Frame

    Start from the business goal and what users actually do.

  2. Specify

    PRD, user stories and acceptance criteria everyone can test against.

  3. Prioritise

    Order the backlog with Business and Development.

  4. Design

    End-to-end flows for customers and for the staff who support them.

  5. Verify

    Implementation review and UAT before each release.

  6. Measure

    Tracking plan, funnels and reports that show whether it worked.

What we measure becomes the next round of backlog items.
